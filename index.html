Tape doesn't last forever.

Most home videos sitting in Joplin-area closets were recorded between the 1980s and early 2000s. That makes those tapes 20 to 45 years old.

Magnetic tape loses color, picture, and sound as it ages. Heat, humidity, and mold speed that up. Snapped tape and cracked shells get more common every year, and working players for formats like Hi8 and VHS-C keep getting harder to find.

Digitizing now captures your footage at the best quality it has left. After that, the copy never fades.

The best time to digitize was ten years ago. The second-best time is this week.

Questions people ask

How much does it cost to convert VHS to digital in Joplin?

At Rewind Joplin, VHS and camcorder tape conversion costs $25 per tape for 1 to 4 tapes, $20 for 5 to 10, $17 for 11 to 25, and $15 for 26 or more. Orders have a $30 minimum, and blank tapes are free.

How long does it take?

Orders of 1 to 4 tapes take 5 to 7 days. Larger orders take 7 to 21 days depending on size. Collections of 26 or more tapes take 14 to 21 days.

Do my tapes ever leave Joplin?

No. You hand your tapes to us in person, we digitize them in Joplin, and you pick them up in person. Nothing gets shipped.

Do you digitize MiniDV or Betamax tapes?

Not right now. Rewind Joplin digitizes VHS, VHS-C, S-VHS, Hi8, Video8, and Digital8 tapes, plus photos, slides, negatives, audio cassettes, and CDs.

What if a tape is blank?

If a tape is completely blank or unrecorded, you pay nothing to digitize it.

Can you fix a broken, snapped, or moldy tape?

Yes. Splicing snapped tape or replacing a cracked shell costs $20 per tape. Cleaning and treating mold or water damage costs $25 per tape.

What file format will I get, and will it play on my phone or TV?

Video becomes MP4 files that play on iPhone, Android, Windows, Mac, and smart TVs. Photos become high-resolution JPEGs. Audio becomes MP3 or WAV files.

Do I get my original tapes back?

Yes. You pick up every original along with your digital files.

Can you make a tribute video for a funeral or anniversary?

Yes. Custom video editing, including trimming, combining clips, and tribute slideshows, costs $60 per hour.

What areas do you serve?

Rewind Joplin serves Joplin, Webb City, Carthage, Carl Junction, Neosho, Duenweg, Oronogo, Galena KS, Pittsburg KS, Miami OK, and the rest of the Four States area. Drop-off and pickup happen by appointment in Joplin.

Is my family's footage kept private?

Yes. Every tape and photo is handled with strict privacy. We keep a backup for 30 days so you can order extra copies.
